Better HUD

The settings Shell Shockers was missing.

Better HUD is a fan-made mod for Shell Shockers. It adds two tabs to the game's Settings, MODS and CROSSHAIR: hide what you don't need, draw your own crosshair, keep the stats of every match, get more FPS on a slow computer and join the server with the lowest ping.

01Crosshair

A crosshair you can see on every map.

The game's crosshair gets lost against bright walls and busy fights. The CROSSHAIR tab lets you draw your own: arms, center dot, outline and color, saved as profiles you can switch between, even one for each gun.

  1. Pick a background like your map's, so the preview looks like a real match.
  2. Drag Length and Width. The preview changes while you drag.
  3. Choose a color. Outline keeps it readable on any wall.
  4. Got a code from a friend, or from Valorant? Import it and give the profile a name.
  • Center dot
  • Crosshair per weapon
  • Profiles & gallery
  • Undo & redo
  • Valorant codes

02Chat

Never miss it when someone talks to you.

In the middle of a fight you don't read chat. With Chat Mentions, your name lights up and you hear a short ping, so you know someone asked you something. Answer by name with @, read the whole match back when you pause, and keep the crosshairs people share.

  1. Someone says your name: it's highlighted and you hear a ping.
  2. Type @ and a letter or two, then Tab puts in the full name.
  3. Pause: every message shows the time it arrived, and old ones are still there.
  4. Hover a crosshair code to see it. Click it to save it as a profile.

03Match History

Every match, saved for later.

Had a great game last night and forgot the code? Every match you finish is saved in your browser with its map, mode, server and every player's kills and deaths. Search it, filter it by mode, map or date, and watch your KDR go up.

  1. Finish a match: it's added to Match History by itself.
  2. Search a player, a map or a game code to find old matches.
  3. Open Progress to see your KDR match by match, and where you play best.
  • Search & calendar

04Share

Post the scoreboard in seconds.

Won a scrim and want proof? Open the match, press Copy as Text and paste it in your group chat. The columns stay lined up, so it reads like a real scoreboard. Prefer a picture? Copy Image draws it in Better HUD's look or as a league result card.

  1. Open the match: both teams, every player, K, D and KDR.
  2. Press Copy as Text.
  3. Paste it in any chat with Ctrl + V.

05FPS & Ping

Smoother games on any computer.

On a laptop, a big fight can drop you under 40 FPS right when it matters. Smart Auto Detail lowers the resolution a little, but only at a safe moment. FPS Check tells you what's slowing the game down, in plain words.

  1. Smart Auto Detail is on from the start and watches your FPS while you play.
  2. A fight drops you under 40 FPS. It waits, so nothing stutters mid-fight.
  3. Once you're cracked, it lowers the resolution a step, and keeps it lower only if that helps.
  4. Still slow? FPS Check finds common causes, like the browser using the weaker graphics card.
  • Smart Auto Detail
  • Render Scale
  • Uncap FPS
  • Disable All Particles
  • FPS Check

06Server Picker

Join the server with the lowest ping.

Lag is often just the wrong region. Server Picker puts every region on the home screen with its live ping, right beside Game Mode. Auto-pick Best Server switches you when another region is clearly faster.

  1. Open Server on the home screen: every region with its ping, live.
  2. Auto-pick moves you to the fastest region, if it beats yours by at least 15 ms or 20%.

And a lot more.

Every setting has a short tip in the game. A few more favorites:

  • GUI ScaleMake the HUD, menus, chat and scoreboard smaller or larger. Your crosshair keeps its size.
  • FOV (Black Bars)See more of the map with black bars at the top and bottom. Only in matches.
  • Legacy Skins & SoundsBring back the classic gun models and the old weapon and game sounds.
  • Auto FullscreenGoes fullscreen like F11 when you click into a match.
  • Tab Out KeyOne key frees your mouse, so you can tab away without your camera snapping.
  • Uncap FPS & Target FPSRun frames back to back while you're spawned in, or cap them at the rate you pick.
  • Disable All ParticlesHide explosion fire, yolk bursts, splats and bullets in one click. Smoke stays visible.
  • Pin Stats on Pause MenuYour kills, deaths and KDR above the weapon select whenever you pause.
  • Kill Bars & Show GunsTeam-colored kill bars and the gun each player got the most kills with.
  • King of the Coop StatsEach player's time on the coop, captures and takeovers on the scoreboard.
  • Stats StyleCopy Image draws a match in Better HUD's look, or as one of 7 league result cards.
  • Backups in ManageSave all your settings and crosshairs to a file, then choose what to restore.

Ready in about a minute.

Better HUD is a userscript: a small add-on that the free Tampermonkey extension runs on the Shell Shockers page.

  1. Get Tampermonkey

    Add the free Tampermonkey extension to your browser. Violentmonkey works too.

    ChromeEdgeFirefoxOpera GX
    Get Tampermonkey

  2. Install Better HUD

    Click Install. Tampermonkey opens its own page: press Install there too.

    Install Greasy Fork

  3. Open the MODS tab

    Go to shellshock.io and open Settings. MODS and CROSSHAIR are the last two tabs.

    Mods Crosshair

Nothing happens in step 3? Chrome, Edge, Brave and Opera need Allow User Scripts turned on for Tampermonkey on the Extensions page.

Updates come by themselves. Tampermonkey checks for new versions, and the bottom of the MODS tab says when you're up to date.

Questions

Is Better HUD free?

Yes. It's free and open source under the MIT license, and the full script is on GitHub and Greasy Fork.

Which browsers does it work in?

Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Opera GX, with Tampermonkey. Violentmonkey works too.

Where are my stats saved?

In your own browser, on your computer. You choose how many matches to keep with Saved Match Limit, and you can export them.

How do I change a setting back?

Every setting has a short tip in the MODS tab. Turn on Show Changed Tab to see only the settings you've changed, each with its own Reset.

Where do I ask for help or a new feature?

On the Better HUD Discord, or open an issue on GitHub.
